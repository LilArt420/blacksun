# 🌑 Black Sun

A solo or party, character-bound instance for Ragnarok Offline, on Mt. Mjolnir under an eclipse 🏔️

The moon is about to swallow the sun over Mt. Mjolnir. And somewhere on the mountain, James Wolf is forgetting his own name 🐺

## 🌑 The Story
James Wolf went hunting in the high ridges of Mt. Mjolnir, like every autumn, with his cousin Nightwing at his side 🏔️
- A pack of werewolves fell on them. Nightwing got James away... but not before a wolf's teeth found his arm 🩸
- The bite closed in a single night. Since then James barely sleeps, eats his meat raw, and his eyes catch the moonlight like a beast's 👁️
- Three nights ago he ran back up the mountain, and Nightwing went after him 🏃
- The old hunters say a **Black Sun** is coming: the night the moon swallows the sun, when every curse of the wolf comes due at once ☀️🌑

His older brother John is waiting in Prontera. If James is still up there when the Black Sun rises, John doesn't know what will come back down 💔

## 🧭 Getting There
Talk to **John Wolf** in Prontera (113, 190), right next to Nightwing's door to the Royal Nightmare 🚪 He tells you his brother's story first, then sends you up the mountain
- Base Level 200+ 🔒
- 90 minutes on the mountain ⏳
- 8 hour cooldown per character, starting when you receive the Black Sun's spoils (a failed climb costs nothing) 🔁

## 🦅 Nightwing, the Wereraptor
Yes, *that* Nightwing 😏 In Prontera he kneels to a Nightmare Queen; on Mt. Mjolnir he's family: James and John are his cousins.
- He carries the Druids' gift, and when the Alpha Werewolves come for you, he takes the shape of a **wereraptor** to fight at your side 🪶
- He keeps that shape for the rest of the night

## ⚔️ The Night, Step by Step
1. 🏔️ **The Crag**: Nightwing meets you at the trail head
2. 🐺 **Horde 1, the Pack**: 450 beasts, 150 each of Moonfang Wolf, Black Sun Shade and Frostbite Hound. Cut down 100 of each
3. 🌕 **The Alpha Werewolves**: the three who bit James surround you with their packs, howling *Join the pack!*... and Nightwing transforms 🦅
4. 🌑 **Horde 2, the Black Sun rising**: the sky darkens and bleeds red. 900 beasts, 300 each of Werewolf, Feral Wereraptor and Black Sun Shade. Cut down 200 of each
5. 🧍 **James**: you find him at last. He begs you to stay away... then howls, and the crag gives way under your feet 😱
6. 🧗 **The Climb**: you land at the foot of the mountain's last slopes. 1,500 beasts, 500 each of Werewolf, Frostbite Hound and Moonfang Wolf, stand frozen all over the mountainside, staring at the Black Sun, until they turn to stare at **you** 👀 350 of each open the summit
7. 🏔️ **The summit**: John followed you up... but James has walked into the dark under the Black Sun, and it has torn the mountain open all the way down to Niflheim
8. 💀 **Niflheim**: the dark field where the Black Werewolf always stood. John begs his brother to come home. James is so tired of fighting... and surrenders to the Black Sun
9. 🔴 **James, the Black Sun**: the strongest werewolf the world has ever known
10. 🌅 **Dawn**: the Black Sun sets. Open the chest where James fell, and he comes back to himself 🐺➡️🧍 Nightwing flies everyone home to Prontera

Every stage's beasts are all out the moment it starts, and every stage holds more than the last 📈 Every beast on the mountain is **big** 🐾 and takes only **10%** of the damage it's dealt 🛡️

While anyone is near James, the Black Sun calls **35 beasts** to his side every 15 seconds 🌀 (his werewolf face flashes up when it does), and **3 Werewolves** in between 🐺🐺🐺 (never while 175+ monsters are already out)

Lost? Whenever there's someone to find, Nightwing tells you where, with a link that opens the navigation 🧭

## 👥 Bring Your Party!
- **Every party gets its own mountain** 🏔️ Any number of groups can climb at once, no waiting for someone else to finish
- The party leader starts the climb at John, and everyone else just talks to John to follow 🧗
- Died? Talk to John again and climb back up while the run is open 🔁
- Only the leader talks to the NPCs, so the story stays together 🗣️
- **Everyone's** kills count ⚔️

## 🎁 The Black Sun's Spoils
When James falls, a chest appears where he lay 📦 **Everyone** in the run opens it for their own share (James only wakes once it's open):
- ⭐ Base and job EXP: 10% of your level each
- 🐺 1 Grey Wolf Cub in Mouth
- 🔮 1 Wolf Orb (Above All) Lv 3
- 📜 5 King Wolf Scrolls
- 🪙 500 Premium Black Coins
- 🎫 1 Premium Black Ticket

Trade the coins and the ticket with John Wolf: 500 Coins for 150 Cash Points 💎, and 1 Ticket for 10,000,000 Zeny 💰

Left before opening it? John mails you your share 📬 One run every 8 hours per character ⏳

## 🖼️ The Portraits
- Nightwing is the same portrait as in Royal Nightmare 🦇 and John and James get their own, in the same style and size 🎨
- James has three, one for each part of the story: himself, the wolf breaking through 🩸, and the Black Sun's werewolf 🐺
- Nightwing's wereraptor form is the client's own Druid art

## 🌌 The Sky
The Black Sun rises as the night goes on: dusk on arrival, a black disc in a burning corona after the alphas, the full blood-red eclipse at the summit, and dawn once James is free 🌅 It travels across the sky faster at every stage ☄️ (a light graphics pass in `client/`, on the instance maps only)

## ⚠️ Monster Skills and Other Mods
The beasts' skills are in `db/mob_skill_db.txt`. The app can combine most tables from several mods, but not this one: if another enabled mod also ships `db/mob_skill_db.txt` (eden-rework-2021 does), only the mod whose folder name sorts last is in effect, and **Settings → Mods** says so under the other one. Black Sun still works without its skills; the beasts just hit without them.

## 💾 Installing
Download `black-sun-v<version>.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page, and in Ragnarok Offline install it with **Settings → Mods → Install from a folder…** Renewal servers only, Ragnarok Offline 1.6.4 or newer.

- 🏷️ Publishing a GitHub Release tagged `v<version>` builds `black-sun-v<version>.zip` ([release.yml](.github/workflows/release.yml)) and attaches it. What changed is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
